The simulated village

This shows how Favor Loop behaves at scale. Up to 150 synthetic members, each with 2 or 3 favors to give and to receive (portrait photos and AI tool setup are the popular asks, and a few members only want to give), arrive a few at a time. They run through the same matcher and the same loop rules as real residents, in a separate database that lives only in memory. Synthetic members say yes most of the time and confirm their favors after a while.

Synthetic members are never counted as real anywhere. The real board and every real number come from real residents only.
