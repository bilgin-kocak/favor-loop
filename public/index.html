Favor Loop

No two of us could trade. The three of us could.

Acan review code and wants a portrait
Btakes portraits and wants a yoga session
Cteaches yoga and wants a code review

No two of them could swap. Favor Loop closes the loop: A reviews C's code, C teaches B yoga, B takes A's portrait. Every favor is free, about 15 minutes, in a public place or online. A loop starts only when all three say yes, and whoever receives a favor confirms it happened.
